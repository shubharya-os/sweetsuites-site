How it works

Park it. Paint it. Place it.

Three steps, one very satisfying loop.

  1. 1

    Slide the cars out

    The hotel's valet lot is gridlocked. Drag cars, SUVs, shuttles and luggage carts along their lanes, and send each one out through the gate that matches its colour.

  2. 2

    Paint the picture

    Every car that exits drives across a pixel-art picture and paints it, cell by cell. Leftover paint waits in your tray, so plan your exits before the tray fills up.

  3. 3

    Renovate the hotel

    Finish a picture and that sofa, bed or pool float moves into your hotel. Spend stars on finishing touches, welcome new guests and raise your hotel's rating.

Features

Made to relax, built to challenge

Gentle first levels that teach themselves, then trickier lots that make you think three moves ahead.

1,000+ puzzle levels

Every level is checked by a solver, so each one can be won. Hard and Super Hard lots turn up regularly to keep you on your toes.

20 hotel areas to restore

From the Lobby and Café to the Pool, the Spa, the Ballroom, the Marina, two Penthouses and a Wedding Garden. Each one fills with furniture you painted yourself.

Tricky lots

New obstacles arrive as you go: frozen cars, locked cars, one-way lanes, barriers, linked cars and tow trucks. Each gets a one-screen how-to first.

Luggage Sort breaks

Every so often a Luggage Sort puzzle drops into the journey: stack the suitcases three to a cart by colour, then it's back to the lot.

Streaks, dailies and events

Valet Streaks hand you boosters for winning in a row. There are daily rewards and tasks, Rush Race and Gold Lot events, and a Valet Pass season.

Offline, no account

Play anywhere: on a plane, on the train, in a dead zone. There is nothing to sign up for. Game Center leaderboards and achievements are there if you want them.

Colour-blind friendly

Every colour has its own roof symbol, and the same symbol is on its gate and its picture cells. No rule in the game depends on telling two hues apart.

  • cherry car, heart symbol
  • sky car, circle symbol
  • lemon car, star symbol
  • lime car, leaf symbol
  • grape car, moon symbol
  • tangerine car, triangle symbol
  • bubblegum car, diamond symbol
  • mint car, drop symbol
  • cocoa car, square symbol

Fair play

Free to play, and straight about it

Sweet Suites is free, supported by optional in-app purchases. No third-party ads, no tracking. Here is how that works.

No surprise paywalls

No sign-up, no paywall and no pop-ups before your first level. The first level is two taps from launch.

Promos stay out of the puzzle

Our in-game promo cards never interrupt a level. Cards that give you a reward, like an extra life, only show when you choose them.

Remove Ads means it

One purchase turns off the between-level promo cards and banners for good. Any purchase at all switches them off too.

No random paid rewards

Everything you can buy shows exactly what you get. There are no loot boxes, and the daily spin is never for sale.
