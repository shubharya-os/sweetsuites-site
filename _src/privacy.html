The short version

  • No account and no server. There is no sign-up and no login, and we have no game server. Your progress is saved only on your iPhone.
  • No third-party ads and no tracking. The game contains no advertising SDK. The only promotional cards it shows are our own, for features inside the game. It does not access your device's advertising identifier and does not ask to track you.
  • Apple handles payments. We never see your card or Apple Account details.
  • Game Center is optional and run by Apple.
  • We do not collect your data. The game's App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.

What stays on your device

The game keeps one save file in its private storage on your iPhone. It holds things like your level, coins, stars, boosters, lives, streaks, hotel progress, event progress, which purchases have been delivered, and your settings (sound, music, haptics, Reduce Motion, notifications).

That save is not sent to us or to anyone else. There is no account and no cloud save. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, Apple's backup may include the save, as it does for most apps; Apple handles that backup under your Apple Account and we have no access to it.

Deleting the game deletes the save. Because we never had a copy, we cannot restore it for you. See Lost progress.

We do not include an analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDK in the game. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous usage statistics and crash reports through App Store Connect. You can change that in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

In-game promotions

The game shows some promotional cards of its own. They promote features inside Sweet Suites itself, are built into the game, and are not provided by any advertising network:

  • Rewarded cards (“watch to continue”): you can choose to view one to get a reward, such as continuing a level or refilling a life. They are always optional.
  • Full-screen cards between levels and banners on the hotel screen that promote in-game features.

These cards are drawn by the game on your iPhone. Showing them involves no ad request, and no data about you or your device is sent to us or to any ad network. Remove Ads turns off the between-level cards and banners, and ad-skip tickets let you skip a rewarded card and still get its reward.

No tracking

The game does not track you. It does not access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), does not show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and does not link anything about you with data from other companies' apps or websites.

Purchases (Apple)

All in-app purchases are made through Apple's App Store and use Apple's StoreKit. Apple processes the payment. Through StoreKit, Apple tells the game on your iPhone which products you bought, so the game can deliver them and so Restore Purchases can bring back Remove Ads. We do not run a server that checks receipts, and we never receive your name, email address, payment details or Apple Account information.

Apple's handling of your purchases is covered by Apple's privacy policy. Apple may share aggregated sales reports with us through App Store Connect.

Game Center

Game Center is optional and is run by Apple. If you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, the game uses it for a highest-level leaderboard, achievements and the weekly tournament. Apple shares your Game Center player identifier and display name with the game for this, and shows your scores to other Game Center players as your Game Center settings allow. Game Center is provided by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policy. We do not store your Game Center data anywhere else. You can sign out in Settings › Game Center.

Opponents in the game's Rush Race events are computer-controlled and labelled as such; they are not real players and no data about you is shared with them.

Notifications

If you turn them on, the game schedules reminders on your iPhone, such as when your lives are full or an event is ending. These are local notifications created by your iPhone itself; we have no server that sends them and they involve no data leaving your device. You can turn them off in Settings › Notifications.

Your rights

Because there is no account and no server, we hold no personal data about you from the game that we can look up, export or delete. Your save is on your iPhone, and deleting the game erases it.

To make a request, email {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} with the subject “Privacy request”. We will reply within one month (or 45 days where the CCPA applies).
